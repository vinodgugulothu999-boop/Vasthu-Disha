# Vastu Disha Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Vastu Disha uses the device camera to provide the Live Vastu Scan experience and uses foreground device-location permission to improve compass/true-heading accuracy. The app does not need background location access.

Camera and heading information are used to provide the requested in-app functionality. Unless a future version explicitly states otherwise, Vastu Disha does not sell personal information or use camera content for advertising.

Users can revoke camera or location permission at any time through their device settings.

For privacy questions, the app owner should publish a support/privacy contact before submitting to Google Play.

This starter policy must be reviewed and replaced with the publisher's final legal/privacy-policy text and a publicly reachable privacy-policy URL before store submission.
